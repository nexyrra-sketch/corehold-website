07Operating Beliefs
B.01

The right technology, not the most technology.

More software is usually the problem wearing the costume of the solution. We measure our work by what it removes as much as by what it adds.

B.02

Ownership is a competitive position.

Two companies can run the same playbook — the one that owns its systems moves faster, spends less over time, and negotiates from strength. Renting is neutral at best. Owning compounds.

B.03

Diagnosis before design. Design before code.

Code written before understanding is expensive guesswork. The investigation is not the preamble to our work — it is the work.

B.04

Systems should compound. Subscriptions expire.

Everything we build is designed to be worth more to you in year five than in year one — accumulating data, intelligence, and fit that no off-the-shelf tool can.

B.05

Fewer clients, deeper systems.

Foundation work can't be done at volume. We take on a small number of engagements and go deep enough that the result outlasts us.
