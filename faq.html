Frequently Asked — Corehold, Dubai

Direct questions, direct answers.

What we build, what ownership actually means, and how an engagement works — answered the way we'd answer them across a table.

What does Corehold do?

+

Corehold is an intelligent systems studio based in Dubai. We design and build custom software, AI-powered systems, automation ecosystems, business infrastructure, digital platforms, and internal tools — owned outright by the client. We replace fragmented subscription stacks with one connected foundation a business controls: the system, the code, the data, and the infrastructure.

What does "ownership" actually mean at Corehold?

+

Literal ownership, not a license tier. At handover the client receives the source code, the data in an open structure, the infrastructure and accounts in their name, full documentation, and the roadmap. Nothing about the system requires Corehold afterwards — clients can maintain it with any team they choose.

How does a Corehold engagement start?

+

Every engagement starts with an audit, not a build quote. We map how the business actually operates — every tool, subscription, handoff, and workaround — then diagnose where money and time leak. Only after that do we design a system. Sometimes the finding is that no new technology is needed; we say that too.

How much does custom software from Corehold cost?

+

It depends on the system, which is why we start with the audit rather than a quote. The comparison we always show is the five-year one: what the current rented stack will cost over five years (often AED 500,000 to 1,000,000+ for mid-size companies — we've published the arithmetic) versus a bounded build investment that ends and leaves the business owning an asset. If the math doesn't favor building, we recommend not building.

Do you only work with businesses in Dubai and the UAE?

+

No. Corehold is based in Dubai (GST, UTC+4) and works with businesses across the UAE, the Gulf region, and worldwide. Engagements run remotely with on-site sessions where useful.

Do you build websites?

+

Yes, when a website is strategically needed — as the front door of an owned system rather than a standalone brochure. We don't sell websites as a product; we build platforms and digital experiences as part of a company's owned infrastructure.

Should my business replace all its SaaS subscriptions?

+

Almost never all of them. Commodity functions like email hosting, accounting, and video calls are usually right to rent. Ownership pays off for core operations — the processes that make a business different — and wherever integration costs, per-seat pricing, or trapped data have made renting the expensive option. The audit distinguishes the two.

Do you use AI in the systems you build?

+

Where it earns its place. We build AI into owned systems for high-volume decisions, document processing, forecasting on the client's own history, and always-on customer response. We don't add AI for the label — if a business's data foundation isn't ready, we say so and fix that first.

What happens after handover — who maintains the system?

+

The client chooses. Many keep Corehold as a long-term partner for evolution and support; others take maintenance in-house or to another team, which the documentation and clean handover are designed to make possible. Ownership means nothing about the system requires us.

How long does it take to build an owned system?

+

The audit takes weeks, not months. Build timelines depend on scope: focused systems commonly ship a first working version within a few months, then grow in stages. We design the smallest system that changes the most, so value lands early rather than at the end of a long project.
